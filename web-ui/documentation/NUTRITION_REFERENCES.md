## Notes on Reference Selection

All references cited in this document are:
- **Peer-reviewed** academic publications or official reports from authoritative scientific bodies
- Published in reputable **journals** (*The American Journal of Clinical Nutrition*, *Medical Clinics of North America*, *British Journal of Nutrition*, *Journal of Sports Sciences*, etc.)
- **Official guidelines** from recognized organizations (Institute of Medicine/National Academies, FAO/WHO/UNU, U.S. Dietary Guidelines)
- Based on **systematic reviews** of scientific evidence and expert consensus

## Key Authoritative Sources

### Institute of Medicine (IOM) / National Academies
The Dietary Reference Intakes (DRI) reports represent the most comprehensive and authoritative nutrition recommendations available. Each report is based on:
- Systematic reviews of scientific literature
- Meta-analyses of clinical trials
- Expert panel consensus
- Consideration of deficiency prevention and chronic disease risk reduction

### FAO/WHO/UNU Expert Consultations
International nutrition standards developed through:
- Global expert panels
- Cross-national data analysis
- Consideration of diverse populations

### U.S. Dietary Guidelines for Americans
Updated every 5 years based on:
- Scientific advisory committee reviews
- Evidence-based methodology
- Public health considerations

## Implementation in Code

The `calculateWeightNutrition()` function in `recommend.js` implements these evidence-based recommendations to provide personalized nutrition guidance based on:
- Individual characteristics (age, gender, weight, height)
- Activity level
- Weight management goals

All numerical values in the code directly correspond to the RDA (Recommended Dietary Allowance) or AI (Adequate Intake) values established in the referenced publications.
